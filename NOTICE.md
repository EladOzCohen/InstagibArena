The MIT license in [LICENSE](LICENSE) covers the code, tools and generated audio in this repository.

**Unreal Engine** and its content belong to Epic Games and are covered by the
[Unreal Engine End User License Agreement](https://www.unrealengine.com/eula).

The **Mannequin characters and animations** used by the game come from Unreal Engine's free content.
They are Epic's content. They ship inside the game builds (as the EULA allows for products made with the engine)
but are not stored in this repository: `Tools/restore_mannequins.ps1` copies them from your own Unreal Engine install.

Sounds and music are generated by `Tools/gen_audio.py` and are not sampled from anything.
